TROUBLESHOOTING:

Q: Emulator slow hai?
   → Virtualization enable karo BIOS mein (VT-x/AMD-V)

Q: Root detection still showing?
   → LSposed properly enable hai? Check karo

   → Module scope mein target app add kiya?

Q: Play Store kaam nahi kar raha?
   → google_apis_playstore image use karo (setup automatically karta hai)
   → First launch pe Play Store setup karo with Google account
